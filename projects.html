About my projects

Having created several projects throughout my journey in programming, I have gained valuable experience in various technologies and methodologies. Although I have developed many smaller programs for personal use, I have also spent time working on larger, more complex projects that have allowed me to apply my skills in more complex, real-world scenarios. For learning purposes, I usually don't use AI agents for building my personal projects.

o-rs-u

O-rs-u is, as of now, my biggest project. It is essentially the popular rhythm game "osu!" rewritten in Rust, with the help of the popular all-code game engine, Bevy.
My original objective was to make an alternative to osu! with better performance, which I partly achieved - it is more stable under load, having better 1% fps, but struggling to keep up in raw fps, which is a limitation of the overkill game engine.
Considering this, I might decide to rewrite my rust version in wgpu to maximize the performance, and to learn something new.
Although some things like a level GUI or certain gameplay gimmicks are missing, the game is still in development, with plans for either custom in-game level browser, or an external level browser (iced gui).
